1. **Phase 0, ingest.** Write `config.json` and `scripts/ingest.py`. Initialise the HyperFrames project in `edit/`

2. **Phase 1, sync.** Write `scripts/sync.py` (envelope cross-correlation, as described in PLAN.md) and produce `edit/sync.json`.

3. **Phase 2, A-roll detection.** Write `scripts/presence.py` to produce `edit/aroll_usable.json` and thumbnails in `.cache/review/`.
   **STOP and show me the candidate ranges (a thumbnail contact sheet is fine) so I can approve or reject each one.**
4. **Phase 3, transcript.** Run Whisper on the Zoom audio, convert the timestamps to master time, and propose chapters with titles and cut points.

5. **Phase 4, build.** Write the first `edit/edl.json` from my approvals, plus `scripts/build.py`. Generate `edit/index.html` and `edit/chapter-NN.html`.

6. **Phase 5, audio.** Normalise the Zoom audio to −16 LUFS (two-pass loudnorm), with a high-pass at 80 Hz and light denoise.
7. **Phase 6, render.** Render each chapter, then concat them into `renders/session-1-final.mp4`. Spot-check sync at the start, middle and end.
